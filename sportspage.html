Snow Sports

Whether you are a first-timer or an experienced rider, there is a snow sport for you. Here are the three most popular ones we plan trips around.

Skiing

Skiing is the classic mountain sport. You glide down slopes on two skis, using poles for balance and turns.

It is a great choice for beginners, because most resorts offer lessons and gentle practice slopes.

Snowboarding

Snowboarding uses a single board, a bit like surfing on snow.

It can take a few days to get comfortable, but many people find it very rewarding once they do.

Snowshoeing

Prefer a slower pace? Snowshoeing lets you walk across deep snow and explore quiet forest trails.

No experience is needed, which makes it ideal for families.

What to Pack

  • Warm, waterproof jacket and trousers
  • Thermal base layers
  • Gloves and a hat
  • Sunglasses or goggles
  • Sun cream (snow reflects the sun)
